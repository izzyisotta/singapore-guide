Split three ways: Cheap (hawker, kopitiam, casual, under S$25 a head), Medium (S$25-90) and Nice (S$90+, the big nights). Walks and runs. Singapore is not a walking city the way London is: it is hot, it rains at 4pm, and the good bits are joined by MRT. But each neighbourhood is very walkable inside itself, and the green spine (Southern Ridges, the Rail Corridor, MacRitchie, East Coast Park) is some of the best urban running anywhere.
